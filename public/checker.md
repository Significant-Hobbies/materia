# Interaction and safety checker

Check a local list of herbs, supplements, and drugs for recorded interactions, duplicate compounds, and shared risks.

The checker runs in the browser against Materia's cited interaction graph. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or guarantee that an unlisted combination is safe.
